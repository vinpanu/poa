Your backup key

This makes the key that locks POA's backup copies, so no one can read them without you. It runs only in this browser: nothing on this page is sent anywhere, and POA never sees your private key.

  1. Tap Make my key.
  2. Copy the private key into your password manager, as a new entry called POA backup private key. Without it no one, you included, can open the backups, so save it before you go on.
  3. Tick the box, then copy the public key and send it to your Project Director. It can lock copies but can't open them.

If you close this page before saving the private key, just make a new key. Nothing changes until your Project Director puts the public key in place.
